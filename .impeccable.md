## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to Sahaj's personal site scroll from his programming work into his interests, getting to know him in increasing detail.

### Brand Personality
Personal, adventurous, playful. The requested warning at the Interests boundary is tongue-in-cheek and invites curiosity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain the established Mondwest/Yport typography, photographic objects, retro windows, and continuous journey rail. The warning is an intentional full-width red-and-white interruption with RGB glitch and tilt driven by scroll progress.

### Design Principles
- Use the journey to reveal more of Sahaj as visitors scroll.
- Make the transition into personal interests a distinct visual moment.
- Preserve legibility while adding playful distortion.
- Support narrow screens and a static reduced-motion presentation.
- Keep scroll effects lightweight and tied to the reader's movement.
