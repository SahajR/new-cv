On Ninenzaka, the Starbucks sign hangs from a wooden building with tiled eaves. Umbrellas fill the narrow street outside; the familiar logo blends into the old Kyoto shopfront.

Inside Kyoto Ninenzaka Yasaka Chaya, the coffee stop moves onto tatami. A cup and a slice of cake sit on a round wooden tray beside a floor cushion — a small change of pace between the streets around Yasaka Pagoda.
