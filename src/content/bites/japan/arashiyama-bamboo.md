The bamboo rises far above the low woven fence along the path in Arashiyama. Closely spaced green stems fill the view, with a little daylight filtering through the leaves overhead.

This photograph catches a pause beside the path, looking up. Later, the scenery opens into the river valley in [Serene Kyoto](/travel/japan/bites/serene-kyoto/).
