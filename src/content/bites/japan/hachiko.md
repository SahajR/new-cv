Hachikō used to wait at Shibuya Station for his owner to return from work. After his owner died, he kept coming back for nine years. The statue remembers that extraordinary habit of waiting.

In this photograph, Hachikō sits in front of a black-and-white view of the old station, with today's Shibuya rising above it.
