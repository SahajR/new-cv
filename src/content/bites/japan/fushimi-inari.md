At Fushimi Inari, the torii line up so closely that the path becomes a corridor of red. Black inscriptions run down the pillars, and each crossbeam frames another gate just beyond it.

Rain darkens the narrow path. An umbrella sits in the middle of the view, while the repeated gates draw the eye further into the passage — one small frame from the morning in Kyoto.
