The Hangzhou photographs include the Olympic Sports Centre's tennis venue, known as the Little Lotus. Its pale, overlapping exterior rises behind the gardens and Asian Games mascots.

There are views from the afternoon and another after dark, when the decorations and surrounding buildings are illuminated. Together they record the area around the venue during the Games.
