At Mutianyu, the Great Wall follows the mountain ridges through a landscape of trees. The photographs alternate between long views of the wall and the stone steps immediately ahead, with watchtowers breaking up the climb.

One frame looks out between battlements and branches; another looks back from a cable car. They give different perspectives on the same hills, from the close stonework to the distant ridgelines.
