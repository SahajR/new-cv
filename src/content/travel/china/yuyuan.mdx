The Yuyuan collection brings together a daytime garden view and the surrounding streets at night. A covered walkway crosses the pond beside an ornate pavilion, with koi beneath it and willow branches at the edge of the frame.

After dark, the camera looks upward. Golden lights trace the layered roofs around the bazaar, while illuminated signs and shopfronts fill the streets below.
