From the Bund, the Pudong skyline stretches across the river, with the Oriental Pearl Tower easy to pick out among the lights. On this side, the camera turns toward an ornate facade glowing above the street.

The next evening's photographs look up from Lujiazui itself. Jin Mao Tower and the Shanghai World Financial Center rise overhead, followed by a closer view of the Oriental Pearl Tower lit in pink and orange.
