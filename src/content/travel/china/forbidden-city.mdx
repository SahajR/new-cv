The Beijing photographs begin beside the Forbidden City's moat, with a corner tower lit up against the night sky. The following afternoon moves inside, between red walls, layered roofs, and bronze guardians standing beside the palace halls.

The wider view comes from Jingshan Park. From above, the separate courtyards and buildings become a pattern of rooftops stretching toward the city beyond.
