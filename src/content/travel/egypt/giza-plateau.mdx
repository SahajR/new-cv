The Giza photographs begin early, with the pyramids beyond the desert ground. There is a camel, a jumping photograph, and a perspective shot that brings a rock in the foreground into the scene.

Later views move closer: first a low wall with the pyramids behind it, then the enormous blocks at the base of a pyramid. These are the outdoor photographs from the plateau, before the camera follows the passage inside the Great Pyramid.
