The museum photographs begin outside, looking from the terrace toward the pyramids. The same shapes that filled the morning's photographs are now distant on the horizon.

Inside the galleries, the frames concentrate on individual objects: a dark jackal figure resting on a shrine, an animal-shaped ceremonial bed, and a painted royal figure behind glass. These evening photographs close the two days recorded in this collection.
