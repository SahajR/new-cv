The morning photographs at Karnak move between sphinxes, monumental gateways, and the columns of the Great Hypostyle Hall. Standing between them gives the camera a useful measure of just how large everything is.

Closer views pick out figures and hieroglyphs in the stone. From a distance the columns read as a repeated pattern; up close, their surfaces are full of detail.
