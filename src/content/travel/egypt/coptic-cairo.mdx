The afternoon photographs turn to Old Cairo. The Hanging Church appears first, followed by details from the Church of Saints Sergius and Bacchus: a glass-covered well and a carved doorway.

An illustrated map on display traces the Holy Family's journey through Egypt. It sits alongside the architectural details in this small collection from Coptic Cairo.
