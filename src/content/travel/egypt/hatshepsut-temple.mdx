At Deir el-Bahari, Hatshepsut's temple stretches across a series of terraces beneath the cliffs. The wide photograph takes in the approach and the whole setting; the next moves alongside a long colonnade.

Inside the closer frames are painted figures, hieroglyphs, and rows of statues. Traces of colour remain visible against the pale stone.
