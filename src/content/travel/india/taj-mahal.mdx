From the garden paths, the Taj Mahal sits beyond rows of clipped trees and visitors. Closer in, its marble dome, tall minarets, and pointed arches fill the frame beneath a cloudy afternoon sky.

An upward view catches the carved details and inlaid borders around the arches. The photographs move between that close view of the marble and the wider setting of the gardens.
