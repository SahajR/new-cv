The photographs at Rajgad begin with a stone gateway perched above the valley. Rough steps lead up through golden grass, while layers of hills fade into the haze beyond the walls.

Further along, the fort follows a narrow ridge between green slopes. Stone steps and ramparts trace the hillside, and the final photograph looks along a grassy path toward the steep rocky summit. These four views were taken across the afternoon of 28 October 2023.
