The Kamakura photographs begin at Kōtoku-in on 6 June, with the Great Buddha filling the background of the morning portrait. A small dessert photographed at a café adds another stop from early that afternoon.

The collection continues at [the seaside station](/travel/japan/kamakurakokomae/), then on to [Enoshima](/travel/japan/enoshima/).
