The Enoshima photographs move between shrine details and the island's shopping streets. There are rows of tied paper fortunes, a stone figure beneath a carved dragon, and a torii surrounded by trees.

Food fills the later frames: three-coloured dango, squid and shellfish on a grill, a clam in its shell, and a soft-serve cone. Together they make a small collection from the afternoon after the seaside railway stop.
