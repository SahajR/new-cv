This photograph puts Mount Fuji above the blue-and-white Lawson storefront near Fujikawaguchiko Town Hall. The shop, parking spaces, and vehicles fill the foreground, with the mountain rising just behind the roofline. It is another view of Fuji from the same afternoon, after the photographs beside the lake.
