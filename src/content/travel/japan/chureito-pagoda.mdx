At Arakurayama Sengen Park, the photographs bring Chureito Pagoda and Mount Fuji into the same frame. The red tiers sit above the trees, with the town spread out between the viewing area and the mountain.

The final photograph looks through a red torii at Arakura Fuji Sengen Shrine. Fuji is still visible beyond it, framed this time by the gate and surrounding branches.
