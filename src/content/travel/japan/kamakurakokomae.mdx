At Kamakurakōkōmae, the railway runs beside the coast. The platform photograph looks along the tracks toward the water, with the road between the station and the shore.

The second frame turns toward the crossing: yellow-and-black barriers, overhead wires, passing traffic, and the sea beyond. The crossing and platform give two views of the same stretch of coast, before the afternoon continues on Enoshima.
