After the afternoon at [Expo ’70 Commemorative Park](/travel/japan/expo-park/), the photographs move to Expo 2025 on Yumeshima. The Osaka Healthcare Pavilion’s sweeping canopy and a large red-and-blue MYAKU-MYAKU display mark the start of the evening.

The smaller details made it into the camera roll too: an Expo emblem underfoot, then a pavilion’s tall columns lit from within as the sky turned pink. These four photographs were taken between 6:03 and 7:22 pm on 1 June.
