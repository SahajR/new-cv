The day around Mount Fuji begins in these photographs at Oishi Park, on the shore of Lake Kawaguchiko. The mountain rises beyond the water, first behind the lakeside sign and then above a spread of blue flowers.

A soft-serve cone makes its way into the next frame, with the gardens and Fuji in the background. The lake and the mountain remain the common thread through all three photographs.
