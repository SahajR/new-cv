Aqaba brings blue water and palm trees into the camera roll, with mountains visible across the waterfront. The photographs continue into the streets, past café fronts and sunlit buildings.

The café table gets a few frames of its own: a green cup, plates of food, and a basket of flatbread. These are the small stops around the waterfront photographs.
