The Wadi Rum photographs begin with the camp at dusk, set between open red sand and dark sandstone mountains. In the morning light, there are tyre tracks, a vehicle crossing the desert, and pale dome tents against the rocks.

Then there is the laptop beside a resting camel. Another frame looks out from the camp lounge, where red chairs face the same wide stretch of desert through the windows.
