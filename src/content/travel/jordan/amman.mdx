Amman fills the frame with pale buildings climbing over one hill after another. These photographs move from the blue dome of King Abdullah I Mosque to the Citadel, where the Umayyad Palace and the columns of the Temple of Hercules stand above the city.

Between the views are photographs from the table: flatbread, small dishes, meat and rice, and a pistachio-covered sweet. They make a second collection alongside the stone buildings and city panoramas.
