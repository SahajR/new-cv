More than 25 kilometres of walking in one day in Petra. The photographs bring together the carved façade of the Monastery and the Treasury, its tall columns framed by the surrounding rose-red rock.

One frame looks up from the visitors gathered beneath the Treasury; another catches it from above, behind a red-and-white headscarf. The people at its base give the stonework its scale.
