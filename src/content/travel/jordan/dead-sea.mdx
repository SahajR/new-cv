Floating in the Dead Sea was one of the memories from this trip. The photographs begin the evening before, with the setting sun reflected across the water beyond the terrace and palm trees.

The next morning’s frames are closer to the shore: a mud-covered hand, a bowl of dark mud, and sunshades along the beach. Beyond them, the water meets a pale blue horizon.
