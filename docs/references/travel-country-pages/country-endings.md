# Country illustrated endings

Five scenes created with the built-in imagegen tool from the user's photographs and approved Japan paper illustration style. Final project assets: `public/images/travel/endings/{jordan,japan,egypt,india,china}.webp`.

The original closing lines live in `src/data/travel-endings.ts`; they are editable draft copy, not quotations attributed to outside authors. `CountryEnding.astro` renders the scene and italic line after each country's stories and Bites. Its own spacing absorbs any extra scroll distance required for the final stop to reach the sticky map; the journal no longer adds a separate blank spacer.

The artwork uses actual transparency, a four-pigment palette, imperfect fine lines, flat acrylic shapes, organic edges and grain within paint. Sharp is used only to resize and encode the generated assets, preserving alpha. No background is removed with code.

Updated 17 September 2026 with the built-in imagegen tool; replaces the seaside station scene at the user's request. Output: `public/images/travel/endings/japan.webp` (960 × 640, alpha preserved).

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset: one transparent editorial scene for the closing footer of a personal travel scrapbook, matching its existing painted maps and country footer illustrations.
Input image 1 is STYLE ONLY: ignore its country map, opaque paper background, and lower-half layout. Input image 2 is the exact SUBJECT photograph: Mount Fuji framed through a red torii at Arakura Fuji Sengen Shrine.
Reinterpret this photograph as a highly simplified handmade acrylic illustration. Preserve the recognizable red torii silhouette, dark slightly upturned top beam, two tall red pillars and red crossbeam, the curved hanging shimenawa rope with a few tassels, and the small distant snow-capped blue Mount Fuji visible centrally through the gate, BELOW its crossbeam. This foreground-gate / distant-mountain relationship is the entire subject. Reduce the foliage to two or three sparse organic branch/leaf shapes framing the sides. Omit the town, secondary gate, dense foliage, plaque writing, and all people.
Delicate slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines. FOUR restrained main pigments extracted from the photograph: muted vermilion, deep olive charcoal, misty slate blue, warm straw cream (for rope and snowy accents). Clearly defined flat acrylic shapes, dry brush marks and rough paper grain ONLY within painted shapes, organic edges. No gradients, photographic shading, or detailed realism.
One complete centered vignette spread across the WHOLE composition, around 75% of canvas width and 75% height, with even margins; landscape 3:2 canvas. Generous open negative space. Keep it legible at 300 pixels wide. The torii should be dominant and Fuji distinctly smaller, just as in the photo.
CRITICAL: genuinely transparent RGBA background outside all painted elements and in all open gaps, including the sky between pillars. No opaque white or cream paper rectangle, no simulated checkerboard, no full sky wash, no border, frame, cast shadow, text, captions, labels, numbers, or watermark.
